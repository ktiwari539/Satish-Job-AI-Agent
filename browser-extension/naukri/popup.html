Naukri Job Capture

Sign in with Google in your normal Chrome browser, open a Naukri job search or job description, then capture only job details.

No Google passwords, tokens, cookies, contact details or profile data are accessed. Nothing runs unless you click. Full-description export opens up to 20 job pages in inactive tabs, reads job text, then closes those tabs. No applications are submitted. Application inspection reports control types, never answers or form values.
